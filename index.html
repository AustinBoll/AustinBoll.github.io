Why It Matters

Motorcycle theft affects entire communities.

Motorcycle theft in apartments, condominiums, and HOA communities is more than an inconvenience — it impacts residents, reputation, retention, and liability.

Residents feel unsafe.

Stolen motorcycles create fear, frustration, and distrust within a community.

Property reputation suffers.

Online theft posts and resident complaints can negatively impact leasing and resident retention.

Liability and headaches increase.

More incidents often mean more complaints, management issues, and avoidable security concerns.

The best time to address motorcycle security is before theft becomes a recurring problem.
Our Story

Built from a real problem. Designed for real communities.

It started in 2016 while living in a Florida apartment complex with no secure motorcycle parking for a 300 lb. Kawasaki Ninja. After learning how common motorcycle theft was in high-density communities, the concern quickly became personal.

Existing security products like disc locks and trackers still allowed a motorcycle to be lifted into a truck or van and disappear in seconds. There had to be a better solution.

Using AutoCAD engineering software, the first MotoKeep design was created with one goal in mind — securely lock a motorcycle directly to the ground.

After months of development, testing, fabrication, anchoring research, and refinement with family support and local fabrication partners, the MotoKeep System was born.

Today, MotoKeep helps HOAs, apartments, condominiums, and property managers provide organized, secure motorcycle parking while helping residents feel safer where they live.
